What We Do

Every formation on Learnify carries the same structure — a theme, a mode, a schedule — so what you see when browsing is exactly what you get when you show up. Registrations are tracked from submission to validation, with a status that updates automatically instead of living in someone's inbox. Whether a session runs présentiel in a training room, en ligne from anywhere, or hybride across both, the process behind it stays the same.

Common Questions

Good to know

How does registration work?

Browse formations, pick one that fits your schedule, and submit your inscription. You'll see its status update as it's reviewed.

What's the difference between the three modes?

Présentiel means in-person, in a physical training room. En ligne is fully remote. Hybride combines both across a formation's sessions.

Can I track my inscription after registering?

Yes — your profile shows every formation you've registered for, along with its current status.

Who do I contact if something looks wrong?

Reach out through the Contact page and an administrator will follow up directly.
